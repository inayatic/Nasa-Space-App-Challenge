How it works. Sun and Moon positions come from Astronomy Engine (MIT licence). The Moon's orientation uses the IAU/WGCCRE rotation model with libration terms (Archinal et al. 2011); our sub-Earth point matches Astronomy Engine's libration to 0.03°. Elevations are for a point on the surface of a 1737.4 km sphere, with the Sun's upper edge counted as lit.
Limits. Site coordinates are approximate crater and massif locations. Terrain is only the single horizon angle you choose, not a real LOLA horizon map, so treat results as planning-level, not mission-grade. All times UTC.
